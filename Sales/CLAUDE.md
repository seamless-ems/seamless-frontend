# Sales — Target Demographic

## Ideal Customer Profile

Recurring multi-speaker conference with a real events budget, a public-facing website, and active social presence. The organiser team is small enough to feel operational pain but big enough to have a tool budget.

## Filters

| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| **Attendee size** | 300 – 3,000 |
| **Speaker count** | 8 – 200 |
| **Format** | Multi-speaker conference (single or multi-track) |
| **Frequency** | Annual or recurring |
| **Ops team size** | 1 – 10 staff |
| **Digital maturity** | Active website + social media presence |
| **Call for speakers** | Nice to have — not a disqualifier if absent |
| **Price point** | $950 USD/yr — needs a real events budget |

## Industry Priority

1. Tech / SaaS / Startup
2. Marketing & Growth
3. Design & Creative
4. Healthcare / Medical
5. Professional Associations (legal, finance, HR)

## Notes

- Upper speaker count is intentionally high (150–200) — larger events may have pain with existing tools or no solution they like
- Enterprise procurement (custom builds, Hopin/Cvent contracts) becomes likely above ~3,000 attendees
- CFP/applications module is a selling point but not a qualifying criterion
