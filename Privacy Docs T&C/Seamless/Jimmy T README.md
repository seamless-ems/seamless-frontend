# Legal Docs — Quick Briefing

## Why Polar changes everything

Polar is our merchant of record. That means **Polar takes the payment, handles VAT, refunds, and chargebacks** — not us. Buyers contract with Polar to buy, then with us to use the product. We don't need a company incorporated to sell through them.

We still need our own docs to govern how people use the platform. That's what the three files here are.

---

## The docs

**Privacy Policy** — tells users what data we collect (organiser accounts, speaker profiles, headshots), why, and their rights. Covers GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA. Contact email is the only public detail.

**Terms & Conditions** — governs use of the platform. Key points: 14-day refund window, beta disclaimer (features can change), organisers are responsible for speaker data they collect, liability is capped.

**Cookie Policy** — Firebase auth cookies (necessary) + Google Analytics (consent only). Short and done.

---

## What we skipped for now

- DPA and Subprocessor List — only needed if an enterprise customer asks
- Acceptable Use Policy — covered in T&Cs section 3
