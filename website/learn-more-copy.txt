WHY WE BUILT IT
────
Having spent 20 years in the events industry, our co-founder James grew increasingly frustrated building workarounds for problems that good software should have solved years ago.

Every year it was the same: no pricing on websites, demos that led nowhere, quotes too large to justify with no way to tailor.

So James teamed up with James and Catherine to build Seamless from scratch, starting with what an organiser actually needs.
